# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participants must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive technical disagreement, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Using inclusive and professional language.
- Respecting different experiences and viewpoints.
- Giving and accepting specific, evidence-based feedback.
- Protecting personal, confidential, and case-related information.
- Prioritizing the safety and accuracy of users.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate misinformation, publication of private information, and other unprofessional behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, review discussions, and other spaces where a person represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact options at https://github.com/alirashidnahal. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and addressed in proportion to their context and impact.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this policy is violated.
