# Expert Wage Calculator 1.1.0

Initial public release of the standalone Expert Wage Calculator WordPress plugin.

## Highlights

- Quick and full shortcodes for official expert wage estimates.
- Versioned tariff catalogs for 1402 and 1405.
- Integer-safe rial calculations with live thousands grouping.
- Specialist subjects, missions, statutory caps, modifiers, and same-field expert panels.
- Exact, range-based, and authority-dependent result states.
- Complete English and Persian PHP and JavaScript translations.
- Responsive, accessible RTL and LTR interface.
- No tracking, external services, Bootstrap, or theme dependency.

## Shortcodes

- `[ik_expert_wage_quick]`
- `[ik_expert_wage_calculator]`

## Requirements

- WordPress 5.6 or later; tested up to WordPress 7.1.
- PHP 7.4 or later.

Calculation results are tariff estimates and do not replace the final amount determined by the responsible authority.
